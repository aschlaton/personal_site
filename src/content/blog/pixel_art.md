Finished my first piece of pixel art! It's a medium that I've loved for a while now thanks to some [games](https://terraria.org/) [I've](https://www.realmofthemadgod.com/) [played](https://www.stardewvalley.net/). It also happens to be a medium that's more approachable (and imo more fun to work with) than others.

Overall pretty satisfied with what I made, which is why it's on the main page of the site right now. Definitely need to learn how to use Aseprite better in the future though; I didn't use a lot of the tools I should've and didn't properly use layers or color indexing like I should've. Regardless it was a pretty enjoyable couple of hours and I really do want to keep working on this kind of stuff.
